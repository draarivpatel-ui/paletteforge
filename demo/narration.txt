PaletteForge is a zero dependency color science and generative poster studio that runs entirely in one page.
Every conversion, harmony, simulation and export is computed locally and deterministically, with no network calls at all.

Colors move exactly between S R G B, linear S R G B, Oklab and O K L C H.
An out of gamut target clips by binary searching chroma at constant hue and lightness, so the clipped color keeps its hue.

Six geometric harmonies generate the palette, from complementary and triadic to a monochromatic tonal scale.
The whole palette scores itself in a W C A G contrast matrix judged on unrounded ratios, so a four point four nine nine can never masquerade as a four point five.

Four seeded composition modes render the poster: flow ribbons, wallpaper symmetry, bauhaus grid and moire superformula.
The same seed always yields a byte identical S V G, and the complete state lives in the U R L hash, so any poster can be shared and restored exactly.

The studio simulates protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia with the Machado two thousand nine matrices, applied to the swatches, the matrix and the poster itself.
A calibration lab grades all six harmonies under full vision and all three simulations, and draws the exact S R G B gamut silhouette in the O K L C H slice at the base hue.

Exports ship the palette as C S S custom properties, a Tailwind config and Figma ready D T C G design tokens.
One S H A 256 digest stamps the audit brief identically on the page, the J S O N, the C S V and the Markdown, and a tamper check proves a single flipped field breaks verification.
